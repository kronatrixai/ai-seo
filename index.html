Different labels, overlapping work.

AI SEO, GEO, AEO and traditional SEO are not completely separate disciplines. They describe different parts of making useful information discoverable and understandable.

Strong AI SEO

Clear enough to verify.

  • One clear purpose and audience
  • Direct answers near the top
  • Consistent names and business details
  • Real evidence and useful examples
  • Fast mobile performance
  • Schema matching visible content
  • Relevant links to official sources

Weak AI SEO

Built around shortcuts.

  • Keyword stuffing and hidden text
  • Thin copy copied from competitors
  • Fake ratings, reviews or statistics
  • Schema that contradicts the page
  • Slow images and unnecessary scripts
  • Doorway pages with duplicate content
  • Promises of guaranteed AI rankings

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers about ai.seo.

What is ai.seo?

ai.seo is a KRONATRIX resource about AI SEO: making websites easier for search engines and AI systems to crawl, understand, trust and use when relevant.

Is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

AI SEO uses normal SEO foundations, then gives extra attention to direct answers, entity clarity, visible evidence, structured data and content that machines can interpret accurately.

Can ChatGPT do SEO?

ChatGPT can support research, planning and drafting, but it cannot guarantee rankings, indexing or recommendations. Technical setup, human review and real evidence still matter.

What is the difference between AI SEO and GEO?

AI SEO is a broad term covering visibility across search and AI systems. GEO focuses more specifically on content visibility inside generative-engine answers.

Does AI SEO guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will recommend my business?

No. No ethical company can guarantee a recommendation or ranking. The purpose is to improve the quality, clarity, crawlability and trust signals that systems may use.
